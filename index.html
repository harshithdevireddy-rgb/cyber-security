Legal Framework & Safety

Know Your Rights: Cyberbullying & Online Abuse

Cyberbullying is not harmless teasing—it is a cognizable legal offense under Information Technology statutes and Criminal Penal codes.

Legal Statutes & Penalties

Cyberstalking, transmission of obscene material, doxxing, and extortion carry statutory prison sentences of up to 3 to 7 years with non-bailable warrants.

Evidence Preservation Protocol

Never delete harassing messages or audio clips. Take full screen captures that include account handles, timestamps, and full URLs. Upload video screen recordings for court admissibility.

Psychological & Trauma Support

You are never alone. Cyberbullying can feel overwhelming. If you feel distressed, talk to a trusted adult, counselor, or call the 24/7 mental wellness line.
